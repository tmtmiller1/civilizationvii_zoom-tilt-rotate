# Changelog

All notable changes to Better Zoom In / Zoom Out are documented here. This project follows semantic versioning.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-10

- First version. Zoom goes six notches past each end of the game's range: in to a 5 degree field of view, and out
  to 90 degrees with the camera turning to look straight down over the map.
- The mouse wheel, a trackpad's two-finger scroll, gamepad triggers and touch pinch all move the view at an even
  pace, and a trackpad scroll stops where you stop.
- Keyboard, screen-edge and gamepad panning slow down as you zoom in, so a step covers the same share of the screen
  at any zoom.
- The view stays where you left it when you open and close the buy-tile and place-building screens, and a zoom after
  the game moves the camera picks up from where the camera is.
