## Zoom, Tilt, & Rotate 1.0.1

Zoom up to 36 times closer than the standard Civilization VII in game zoom allows, zoom out to a top-down view of the
map, and tilt and turn the camera to face any direction. No snap-back: your close-up holds for as long as you want it.

### What's in 1.0.1

The license now says that Civilization VII and its art and data belong to Firaxis and 2K. The mod itself plays exactly
as in 1.0.0.

### Why I made it

Before building this, I tested the zoom mods already on the Workshop. The closest any of them got was about 2.3 times
closer than the game, one let go of its close-up after 4 seconds, and neither let you set the camera angle or keep a
heading other than north. One of them put this down to the engine: that it forces the close-up to snap back, and that it
can't report or set a heading.

I measured what the engine actually does:

- **The snap-back is a timer in the mod**, not the engine. Raise that mod's own hold setting to 20 seconds and the
  close-up holds 20 seconds. Without a timer, a narrow lens holds for as long as you leave it.
- **The engine takes a field of view from 1 to 120 degrees** and holds every value in between. The 25-degree floor that
  mod stops at is a constant in its own code.
- **The camera reports its heading** in the same state the mod already reads its zoom from, and the engine's rotate call
  holds any heading you give it.
- **The engine's tilt call holds any angle** from straight down to 80 degrees toward the horizon.

That's the room this mod works in, and it uses all of it.

| | Unmodded | Mod A | Mod B | Zoom, Tilt, & Rotate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Closest lens | 35° | 25°, for 4 seconds | 15° | 5°, 2° or 1° (7x, 18x or 36x closer) |
| Farthest lens | 35° | 60°, for 4 seconds | 60° | 90°, looking straight down |
| Close-up holds | — | no | yes | yes |
| Trackpad swipe keeps the close-up | — | no | no | yes |
| Gamepad trigger zooms | yes | no | yes | yes |
| Set the camera angle | — | no | no | 0 to 80°, held |
| Face any heading and keep it | — | no, swings back to north | no | yes |

Measured in game 1.5.0 with every other mod off, using real macOS input. The full write-up, with pan speeds, city
screens and the other problems found, is in
[docs/compared-with-other-zoom-mods.md](https://github.com/tmtmiller1/civilizationvii_zoom-tilt-rotate/blob/main/docs/compared-with-other-zoom-mods.md).

### Recent releases

#### 1.0.0

- Six extra notches of zoom past each end of the game's range. How close you can get is in Options > Add-ons: Closer
  (7x, the default), Much closer (18x) or All the way (36x).
- Tip the camera from straight down to nearly level, and turn it to face any direction. A tap moves one step onto a
  round number; holding glides and stops where you let go. Back to normal returns to north, the game's angle and the
  game's zoom range in one go.
- Mouse wheel, trackpad, gamepad triggers and touch pinch all work, and a trackpad scroll stops where you stop.
- A camera panel next to the minimap with a zoom gauge, a compass and an angle gauge you can drag, plus optional dials
  on screen.
- Panning keeps the same pace on screen at any zoom and follows the screen when the view is turned.
- Your zoom, angle and heading come back after the buy-tile and place-building screens.
- Keys out of the box: Shift with the arrow keys, Shift + 0 and Shift + C. Change them in Options > Add-ons > Zoom,
  Tilt, & Rotate > Camera keys.
- In 12 languages.

### Install

Download `zoom-tilt-rotate-v1.0.1.zip` below and unzip it into the game's `Mods` folder, so the `zoom-tilt-rotate`
folder sits directly inside it. Then turn on Zoom, Tilt, & Rotate in the game's Add-ons screen.

- macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Civilization VII/Mods/`
- Windows: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Firaxis Games\Sid Meier's Civilization VII\Mods\`

Made for Civilization VII 1.5.0. Your saves aren't touched, so you can turn it on or off at any time.

The logo and the screenshots are attached as `-logos.zip` and `-screenshots.zip`.
